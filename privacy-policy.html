Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7/13/2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Key Investment Group Transaction Categorizer ("the App") collects, uses, and protects data. The App is an internal tool built by and for Key Investment Group to automate categorization of financial transactions in our QuickBooks Online company file. It is not offered to the public or to any third party.

1. Who this applies to

This App is used solely by authorized employees and contractors of Key Investment Group. It is not a consumer-facing product.

2. Data we access

Through the QuickBooks Online API, the App accesses transaction-level accounting data belonging to Key Investment Group's own QuickBooks Online company file, including but not limited to: bank and credit card transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, payee names, account names, and chart-of-accounts categories.

3. How we use this data

Data is used exclusively to:

  • Identify patterns in historical transaction categorization
  • Automatically categorize new transactions with high confidence
  • Present uncertain transactions to internal staff for manual review via a private Google Sheet
  • Write confirmed categorizations back to the QuickBooks Online company file

4. Data storage and sharing

Transaction data used by the App is processed and stored only within:

  • Key Investment Group's QuickBooks Online company file
  • A private Google Sheet accessible only to authorized Key Investment Group staff
  • Cloud infrastructure operated by or on behalf of Key Investment Group (e.g., Google Cloud Platform) used solely to run the categorization process

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any third party. Data is not used for advertising or any purpose other than internal accounting automation.

5. Data retention

Historical transaction data is retained only as long as needed to support categorization accuracy and is deleted or overwritten as part of normal operation. QuickBooks Online remains the system of record for all financial data.

6. Security

Access to the App's infrastructure, credentials, and the review Google Sheet is restricted to authorized Key Investment Group personnel. QuickBooks Online API access is authenticated via OAuth 2.0, and credentials are stored securely and not shared with unauthorized parties.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to accounting@keyinvestmentgroupllc.com .
